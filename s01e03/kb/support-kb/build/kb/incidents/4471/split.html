How to tell them apart

Not by subject: all three share the same subject lines. Not by intake’s tag: it guesses from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant. Look at the tenant, the way in, and the body.

  • parser: a day-first tenant, by app, email or the booking-v2 form. The body names two dates with day and month swapped: “8 December is now 12 August”.
  • hint: a booking-v3 tenant (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour), by the form. The body looks the same as parser, but the hint under the date field told the person to type month first. Fixing the parser leaves this one as it is; the form’s hint or its reading has to change.
  • shift: the date moves by one day, or differs between two places: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” No month swap explains it. The desk had already noted these (desk notes, oneDayMove). They stay counted under parser, where the desk files them by tenant, and they have a ticket of their own.
