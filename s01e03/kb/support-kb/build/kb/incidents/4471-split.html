How to tell them apart

  • Do not use intake’s tag. It comes from the reporter’s browser. It tags 42 reports as hint. Only 33 are.
  • Use the tenant and the channel. Day-first tenant, form channel, booking-v3 form: hint. Day-first tenant, any other way in: parser.

What we are not sure of

Five parser reports (R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620) are one day off, not day and month swapped. One mentions a client in Lisbon who got Tuesday while a client in Toronto got Monday. That may be a time-zone effect. We did not give it its own ticket: the desk files them under parser, and the cause is not proven. Check them again after the parser fix.

Lessons

  • Merging by subject line puts different bugs in one ticket. Read the body before you merge.
  • Say which count you use. The desk counts by tenant and channel.
