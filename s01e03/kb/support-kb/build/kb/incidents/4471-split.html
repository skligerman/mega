#4471 holds 412 unique reports (the export has 427 rows; 15 ids repeat). They are not one bug. They are three problems with three different fixes. Counts are the desk’s own view (its cause table, below).

The queued fix covers only the first problem. It does not fix the other two.

How to tell them apart

  • Do not use intake’s tag. It comes from the reporter’s browser. It tags 42 reports as hint. Only 33 are.
  • Use the tenant and the channel. Day-first tenant, form channel, booking-v3 form: hint. Day-first tenant, any other way in: parser.
  • Read the body for a shift. Swapped day and month: parser or hint. One day off, with no swap: shift. The desk’s own oneDayMove note names the 5 reports: R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620.

What we are not sure of

The shift cause is a lead, not proven. The reports mention June only, and a client in Lisbon who got Tuesday while a client in Toronto got Monday. That looks like time zones. Four other vague reports (R-41290, R-41417, R-41514, R-41615) stay in parser.

Lessons

  • Merging by subject line puts different bugs in one ticket. Read the body before you merge.
  • Say which count you use. The desk counts by tenant and channel, so a report can sit in two tickets.
